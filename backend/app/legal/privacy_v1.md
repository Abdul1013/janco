# JANCO Privacy Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Last updated:** 11 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JANCO ("we", "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal data when you use the JANCO mobile application, website, and related services (the "Platform"). We are committed to handling your data in accordance with the **Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023 (NDPA)** and the regulations of the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

By using the Platform you consent to the practices described here.

---

## 1. Who This Applies To

This Policy applies to everyone who uses JANCO, including **Customers** (people who book services) and **Janitors** (independent providers who perform them).

## 2. Personal Data We Collect

**Information you give us:**
- **Account details:** email address, full name, password (stored only as a secure hash).
- **Profile details (optional):** phone number, profile photo, home/service address, and a landmark description.
- **Location:** GPS coordinates (latitude/longitude) used to match you with nearby providers, calculate distance-based pricing, and support dispatch.
- **Janitor onboarding:** service types offered, experience, bio, and identity verification data (see Section 4).
- **Content you create:** bookings, ratings, reviews, and support messages.

**Information collected automatically:**
- **Device & session data:** device/user-agent information and IP address associated with your login sessions, used for security and fraud prevention.
- **Push notification token:** if you enable notifications, an Expo/FCM push token so we can send you booking and account updates.

## 3. How We Use Your Data

We use personal data to:
- create and manage your account and authenticate you securely;
- match Customers with Janitors and facilitate bookings;
- calculate pricing and process payments;
- verify Janitor identities and maintain trust and safety;
- send transactional messages (booking updates, password resets) and — where relevant — service updates and promotions;
- provide customer support and resolve disputes;
- detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, and security incidents;
- comply with legal obligations.

## 4. Identity Verification (Janitors)

Janitor verification is performed by our KYC partner **Dojah**. Verification may require a government identifier (**NIN** or **BVN**) and a liveness/selfie check. **We do not store your raw NIN or BVN number.** We retain only the verification **status** and a reference token returned by the provider. The identifier and biometric check are processed by Dojah under its own terms and privacy practices.

## 5. Payments

Payments are processed by **Paystack**. Card and payment details are handled by Paystack and are **not stored on JANCO's servers**. We retain transaction metadata (such as amount, status, and a payment reference) necessary to operate the Platform and reconcile bookings.

## 6. Third-Party Processors

We share data with service providers only as needed to operate the Platform:

| Processor | Purpose | Data shared |
|---|---|---|
| Paystack | Payment processing | Payment amount, reference, contact email |
| Dojah | Identity/KYC verification (Janitors) | Government identifier, liveness image, name |
| Expo / Firebase Cloud Messaging | Push notifications | Push token, message content |
| Cloudinary | Image hosting (avatars, room scans) | Uploaded images |
| Neon / PostgreSQL (hosting) | Database hosting | Stored account and booking data |

We do not sell your personal data.

## 7. Marketing Communications

With your consent, we may send you updates and promotions by email or push notification. You can opt out of promotional messages at any time (for example, by disabling notifications or using the unsubscribe option). Transactional and account-security messages are not promotional and may still be sent.

## 8. Data Retention

We keep personal data for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Platform, comply with legal and tax obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

## 9. Your Rights Under the NDPA

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:
- **access** the personal data we hold about you;
- **correct** inaccurate or incomplete data;
- **request deletion** of your data ("right to be forgotten");
- **object to or restrict** certain processing;
- **withdraw consent** where processing is based on consent;
- **data portability** for data you provided to us;
- **lodge a complaint** with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

To exercise these rights, contact us at **privacy@janco.app**. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

## 10. Security

We protect your data using measures including encrypted password storage (bcrypt), access controls, role-based authorization, and audit logging of sensitive actions. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and to respond promptly to incidents.

## 11. Children

The Platform is not directed to children under **18**, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us so we can remove it.

## 12. International Transfers

Some of our processors may store or process data outside Nigeria. Where data is transferred internationally, we take steps to ensure it receives a level of protection consistent with the NDPA.

## 13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the version number and "Last updated" date and may notify you through the Platform.

## 14. Contact

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact our data protection contact at **privacy@janco.app**.
